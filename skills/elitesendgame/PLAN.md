# Elites Endgame: dark-humour reels (owner, 12 Nov 2026)

Channel identity: the people who secretly run things. Secret societies, mafia, rich families, money, conspiracy theories.
Look: dark moonlit 3D (story3d.js: k.night, fog, capStyle 'film', R.light moon), elite:* cast only (Quaternius Ultimate Modular
Men/Women, never the toy Superhero bodies), newspaper/document inserts (k.paper). Voice: Cartesia 627d4560-d254-4a18-90b5-76e3362e9483, one take.

## Rules for every reel
1. Hook: one or two sentences, under 25 words, weird from the first three words, with a double meaning. The first shot shows the
   dirty or scary reading; the real meaning lands by second 3.
2. "You" and present tense where it fits. Short sentences. A full stop before each punchline so the voice pauses.
3. A new shot or reveal every 3-5 seconds; the camera always drifts (push-in, orbit, crane). Faces close-up at reveals.
4. Escalate: three beats, each worse or stranger than the last. Absurd twist with a gag (BOOM card, missile, launch, running away) at ~70%.
5. One dark true sting near the end (the fact that makes people comment).
6. End with a tag-a-friend line, then a half sentence that loops into the hook so it replays cleanly.
7. Under 60 s (about 140 words). Every fact true and in meta.json sources. Conspiracies told as origin story + what is real, roasted.
   Never the antisemitic "banker family runs the world" myths.
