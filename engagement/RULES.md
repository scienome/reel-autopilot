# Engagement rules (every series)

Every reel is built to be watched to the end, watched again, commented on and shared. Check each one against this
list before pushing.

1. **Hook in the first second.** The first spoken line and the first frame carry the reel: open in the middle of the
   action, on the most surprising image, with on-screen text that states the hook. No slow intros, no "in this video".
2. **Something changes every 2-3 seconds:** a cut, a pop-up label, a reaction, a sound hit. Nothing sits still.
3. **Open a loop early, close it late.** Make the viewer need the ending ("nobody saw the twist coming", "Dave did not
   read page two"). The twist or punchline lands in the last 5 seconds, never in the middle.
4. **Loop the ending into the start.** The last line or image should lead naturally back into the first, so the
   replay feels seamless. Replays are the strongest signal there is.
5. **20-35 seconds.** Shorter reels get finished more often. Cut every line that is merely nice.
6. **The caption ends with a two-sided question** (A or B? who in your house? wrong answers only?) or "tag the one
   who..." for relatable reels. Real questions beat "comment YES" style bait, which the platforms push down.
7. **The first comment** (`engagement/<series>.json`, or `first_comment` in the reel's meta.json) is a hot take or
   a playful vote that people want to argue with. One line, an emoji or two.
8. **Make it shareable:** relatable ("that's literally us"), unbelievable but true ("wait, that's REAL?"), or a debate
   people want to drag a friend into.
9. **Series and parts** (Part 1/3) now and then, ending on a cliffhanger, so people follow for the next one.
10. **Cover frame (`cover_t`)** on the most surprising frame, with the hook text readable.
