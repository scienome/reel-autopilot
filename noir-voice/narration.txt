The Illuminati were real. But not the way you've heard. May first, seventeen seventy-six. Bavaria. One professor. One small, secret group. At their peak, they stretched from Italy to Denmark. From Warsaw to Paris. Then, in seventeen eighty-four, secret societies were banned. After seventeen eighty-five, the record goes silent. Nine years. That's all the record shows. Then, in seventeen ninety-seven, a book appeared. It claimed they never died. Writers even blamed them for the French Revolution. Most historians disagree. Two hundred and forty years later, we're still looking for them. Truth, or just a good story? Next secret: the lizard people.
